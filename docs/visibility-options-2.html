Recommendation: B with D's colour — a teal open eye at the trailing edge, a struck eye in grey when hidden, the row dimmed, and a hatch behind it. Four signals, no width taken from the name, and nothing on the left but the layer.

The teal matters more than it looks. Blue is selection in this interface — the selected row, the accent edge, the focus ring — and if "drawn" is also blue then a selected hidden layer and an unselected drawn one carry the same colour in different places. Teal is already the imagery panel's word for "this is what is on the map", so it is a colour the application has already taught.

One thing I would add whichever you pick: alt-click the eye to hide every other layer, the way Photoshop and QGIS both do. It is the single most-used shortcut in a table of contents and costs nothing but a modifier check.
